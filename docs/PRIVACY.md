# Privacy Policy — Amal School

**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

## 1. About this policy

Amal School is operated by **AMAL SCHOOL COMMUNITY INTEREST COMPANY**, a community interest company limited by guarantee and registered in England and Wales with company number **17421047** ("Amal School", "we", "us"). The company is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. Our registered office is **3 Pinedene Carlton Grove, London, England, SE15 2UL**.

We provide online Maths and English learning for Sudanese children, including lessons, interactive practice, progress tracking, and teacher support.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, who may receive it, and the choices available to students, parents, caregivers, teachers, and volunteers.

Because children use Amal School, we aim to use clear language, collect only what is needed, and place the child's best interests first.

## 2. Information we collect

### Parent and caregiver applications

We may collect:

- Parent or caregiver name, occupation, WhatsApp number, and optional email address
- Whether the family is of Sudanese descent
- Whether a child has been affected by the war in Sudan or missed significant schooling
- Details about time out of school or displacement-related circumstances
- The number and ages of eligible children
- Information about access to a phone, the website, or Zoom lessons
- Programme consent and acceptance of our terms

Some eligibility information may be sensitive. We use it only to assess eligibility, understand support needs, plan delivery, and protect children. It is not used for advertising or made public.

### Student information

We may collect:

- Name, age or grade level, language preference, and account details
- Parent or caregiver contact and consent information where required
- Lesson viewing progress and completion
- Practice answers, attempts, scores, feedback, and uploaded work
- Diagnostic assessment results and learning recommendations
- Messages sent to learning-support or AI features
- Basic device, browser, security, and service logs

### Teachers and volunteers

We may collect:

- Name, verified email address, WhatsApp number, and city or country
- Educational background and areas of interest
- Availability and expected weekly commitment
- Lessons, content, feedback, and operational activity created through the platform

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Create and secure accounts
- Review programme eligibility and enrol learners
- Deliver lessons, Practice activities, teacher support, and Zoom-based learning
- Save progress, mark answers, unlock lessons, and show learning feedback
- Help teachers understand learner progress and provide support
- Communicate with parents, caregivers, teachers, and volunteers
- Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the platform
- Detect misuse and respond to safeguarding concerns
- Improve content and service performance using appropriately limited or aggregated information
- Meet legal, regulatory, safeguarding, and partner-reporting obligations

We do not sell children's personal information or use it for behavioural advertising.

## 4. Our reasons for using information

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 where they apply, we rely on:

- Consent, including parent or caregiver consent where required
- Performing a contract or taking steps requested by a user or family before providing the educational service
- Our legitimate interests in operating, improving, and securing a free educational service, where those interests do not override a child's rights
- Safeguarding, vital interests, legal obligations, and regulatory requirements where applicable

Consent can be withdrawn by contacting us, although this may mean that some services can no longer be provided.

## 5. Children and parent or caregiver involvement

Parents and caregivers should provide accurate information and help children use the platform safely. We may ask for confirmation of parental responsibility or identity before acting on a request involving a child's account.

We use privacy-protective defaults and do not provide public student profiles, behavioural advertising, or public social features. Children should not submit home addresses, identity documents, financial details, or other information that is not requested.

## 6. Who we share information with

We share information only where needed with:

- Hosting, database, authentication, email, video, and technical service providers
- AI service providers when an AI-supported feature is used
- Teachers, authorised volunteers, and administrators supporting the relevant learner or programme
- Partner organisations and refugee-camp education teams involved in programme delivery
- Professional advisers, regulators, safeguarding bodies, or law enforcement where required or necessary to protect someone

Partners normally receive the minimum information needed for delivery or aggregated reporting. Identifiable learner information is shared only where there is a valid reason and appropriate permission or legal basis.

Some providers may process information in another country. Where required, we use appropriate contractual and organisational safeguards for international transfers.

## 7. WhatsApp and external services

If a parent, teacher, or volunteer chooses WhatsApp as a contact method, their phone number and messages are also processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Zoom and other linked services likewise operate under their own policies.

## 8. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for education, safeguarding, account administration, partner obligations, dispute handling, and legal requirements. Retention depends on the type of record and the learner's involvement in the programme.

When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it. Limited copies may remain temporarily in secure backups.

## 9. Security

We use access controls, authentication, encrypted connections, restricted staff access, and other reasonable technical and organisational measures. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please contact us immediately if you believe an account or child's information is at risk.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Ask for a copy of personal information
- Correct incomplete or inaccurate information
- Ask us to delete information
- Restrict or object to certain uses
- Withdraw consent
- Receive portable information where applicable
- Ask for human review of a significant automated decision
- Complain to the relevant data protection authority

Children may hold these rights themselves. A parent or caregiver may exercise them where appropriate. Some rights are not absolute, and we may need to retain limited information for safeguarding or legal reasons.

Send requests to [admin@amalschool.org](mailto:admin@amalschool.org). We may ask for information needed to verify identity and authority. We aim to respond without undue delay and within the period required by applicable law.

Where UK data protection law applies, you may also complain to the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.

## 11. Cookies

We use essential browser storage and cookies for sign-in, language settings, security, and core platform functions. If optional analytics are introduced, we will provide appropriate notice and controls before using them where consent is required.

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. Material changes will be highlighted on the platform or sent to the relevant account contact.
